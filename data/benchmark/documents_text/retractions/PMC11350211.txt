Retraction Note: Mechanism of arterial injury exacerbated by hyperhomocysteinemia in spontaneously hypertensive rats Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-023-28731-9, published online 11 February 2023 The Editors have retracted this Article. An investigation by the journal found that the data used in this study has been previously published 1 in an article with shared authors. Figure 2, Figure 5, and Figure 6 of the Article display the same data as Figure 2, Figure 4, and Figure 5 of 1 , respectively. Overlap was also noted between images in Figure 3C of the Article and Figure 3 (B1) of the previous publication 1 . None of the Authors have responded to correspondence from the Editors about this retraction.
