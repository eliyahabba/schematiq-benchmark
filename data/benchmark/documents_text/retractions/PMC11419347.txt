Retraction: Screening and functional p rediction of differentially expressed genes in walnut endocarp during hardening period based on deep neural network under agricultural internet of things The PLOS ONE Editors retract this article [ 1 ] because it was identified as one of a series of submissions for which we have concerns about peer review integrity and similarities across articles.
