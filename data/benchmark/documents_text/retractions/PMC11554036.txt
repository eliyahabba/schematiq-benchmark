Retraction: Exploring rural inhabitants’ perceptions towards food wastage during COVID-19 lockdowns: Implications for food security in Pakistan The PLOS ONE Editors retract this article [ 1 ] because it was identified as one of a series of submissions for which we have concerns about authorship, ethics approval, integrity of the underlying data, reliability of the published results, and peer review. We regret that the issues were not identified prior to the article’s publication. GRM did not agree with the retraction. JZ and MAA either did not respond directly or could not be reached. Reference 1. Zhao J, Madni GR, Anwar MA (2022) Exploring rural inhabitants’ perceptions towards food wastage during COVID-19 lockdowns: Implications for food security in Pakistan. PLoS ONE 17(3): e0264534. 10.1371/journal.pone.0264534
