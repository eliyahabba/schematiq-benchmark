Retraction Note: Extremely efficient aerogels of graphene oxide/graphene oxide nanoribbons/sodium alginate for uranium removal from wastewater solution Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-52043-1, published online 13 January 2024 The Editors have retracted this Article. After publication, concerns about unusual patterns in the XRD data presented in Figure 2 were brought to the attention of the Editors. Upon review, the Editors no longer have confidence in the reliability of the results and findings of this Article. All Authors disagree with the retraction. Contributor Information Ali A.
