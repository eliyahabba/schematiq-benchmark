Retraction Note: Relationship between melatonin receptor 1B and insulin receptor substrate 1 polymorphisms with gestational diabetes mellitus: a systematic review and meta-analysis Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/srep06113, published online 22 August 2014 The Editors have retracted this Article. Following publication, the misnaming of the Begg’s funnel plot as “Begger’s” in the Article was brought to the attention of the Editors. Investigation by the Editors have uncovered further issues, including significant textual overlaps with previously-published work by different authors such as [ 1 ]. The Authors have not responded to the Editors’ requests for clarification and the Editors no longer have confidence in the claims of the Article. The Authors did not respond to correspondence from the Editors about this retraction. Footnotes Yan Zhang and Cheng-Ming Sun contributed equally. Reference 1. Liu, M. et al. Apolipoprotein E gene polymorphism and Alzheimer’s disease in Chinese population: a meta-analysis. Sci. Rep. 4 , 4383. 10.1038/srep04383 (2014).
