Retraction Note: Identification of RNA processing factor LSM5 as a new adverse biomarker in nasopharyngeal carcinoma Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-94968-1, published online 22 March 2025 The Editors have retracted this Article. After publication, concerns were raised regarding the authenticity of the cell lines used in the experiments. The STR profiles provided by the Authors confirmed that the cell lines were contaminated with HeLa cervical carcinoma cells and their derivatives. In addition, image duplication was identified in Figure 7K, and the original data could not be verified. The Editors therefore no longer have confidence in the results and conclusions reported.
