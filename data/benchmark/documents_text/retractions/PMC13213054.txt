Retraction Note: Comparative plastome analysis of Schweinfurthia papilionacea and Schweinfurthia imbricata clarifying their taxonomic position in Lamiales Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-22748-y, published online 05 November 2025 The Editors have retracted this Article. After publication, concerns were raised regarding the scientific validity of the reported Ka/Ks ratios (higher than 5–10), which formed a key piece of evidence in the study. The Authors were asked to clarify their calculation methods. Although the Authors could reproduce their original results using the reported model, they subsequently performed an additional analysis using a different model on the same alignment and obtained substantially different Ka/Ks values (below 0.15). This demonstrates that the original results are highly sensitive to the choice of model and cannot be relied upon as robust evidence. The Editors have therefore lost confidence in the reliability of the findings of this Article. All Authors disagree with the retraction.
