Retraction Note: Spin polarized first principles study of electro-magnetic and optical properties of K 2 NaXI 6 (X :Cr Fe) double halide perovskites Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-42192-w, published online 27 March 2026 The Editors have retracted this Article. After publication, concerns have been raised that the work described in this Article was not performed by the Authors and is based on material from another researcher’s unpublished work to which the Authors had confidential access. The Editors therefore have no confidence in the authorship of this Article. All Authors disagree with this retraction.
