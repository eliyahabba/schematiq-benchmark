Retraction Note: Heart transplantation: comparing the impact of modified heart preservation with conventional methods Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-87091-8, published online 23 January 2025. The Editors have retracted this Article. Following publication, concerns were raised about errors in the statistical analysis, as well as irreconcilable discrepancies in the data. In addition, the Editors requested the provision of the ethical approval documentation, but the Authors were not able to do that. The Editors therefore no longer have confidence in the research presented in this Article. Nianguo Dong agrees with the retraction and its wording. Xiaoke Shang, Changdong Zhang, Yixuan Wang, Mei Liu, and Yucheng Zhong did not respond to the Editors’ correspondence about this retraction.
