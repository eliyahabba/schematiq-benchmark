Retraction: Prioritizing robots in intelligent manufacturing using q-rung orthopair fuzzy decision-making method and unknown weight information The PLOS One Editors retract this article [ 1 ] due to concerns about potential manipulation of the publication process. These concerns call into question the validity and provenance of the reported results. We regret that the issues were not identified prior to the article’s publication. Additionally, Reference 47 in [ 1 ] is retracted. All authors either did not respond directly or could not be reached. Reference 1. Sheng M, Zhu H, He Y, Wang S, Zhou G.
