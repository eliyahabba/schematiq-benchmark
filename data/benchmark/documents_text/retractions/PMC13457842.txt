Retraction Note: The effectiveness of microlearning-based education on medication adherence and health literacy in elderly individuals: a randomized controlled trial Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-29769-7, published online 22 November 2025 The Editors have retracted this Article. After publication, concerns were raised that the study relies on the Morisky Medication Adherence Scale, which was retracted in 2023 1 . When queried about this, the Authors indicated that the study uses a Persian translation of the scale, validated in the work where it was first reported 2 . However, the investigation of that study by the Editors revealed that it did not follow the best practices for translation and validation of such tools: the study does not mention multiple forward translation by independent translators, reconciliation and synthesis of those translations, or back translations. Additionally, it does not perform test-retest reliability metrics, construct validity testing, or convergent and discriminant validity testing. Given these concerns about the validity of the translated scale, and the issues with the original version of the MMAS, the Editors no longer have confidence in the results of this Article. Jamileh Mokhtari Nouri did not explicitly state if they agree or disagree with this retraction. Hossein Poorcheraghi did not respond to the correspondence from the Editors about the retraction.
