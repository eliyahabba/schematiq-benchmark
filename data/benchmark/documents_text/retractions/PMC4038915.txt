Retracted: Process Evaluation of a Positive Youth Development Program in Hong Kong Based on Different Cohorts The paper titled “Process Evaluation of a Positive Youth Development Program in Hong Kong Based on Different Cohorts” [ 1 ], published in The Scientific World Journal, has been retracted as it is found to contain a substantial amount of material, without referencing, from the published article titled “Process Evaluation of a Positive Youth Development Program: Project P.A.T.H.S,” Ben M. F. Law and Daniel T. L. Shek, Research on Social Work Practice, vol. 21, no. 5, pp. 539–548, 2011.
