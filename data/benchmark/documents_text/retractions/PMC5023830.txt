Retracted: Superoxide-Dismutase Deficient Mutants in Common Beans ( Phaseolus vulgaris L.): Genetic Control, Differential Expressions of Isozymes, and Sensitivity to Arsenic BioMed Research International has retracted the article titled “Superoxide-Dismutase Deficient Mutants in Common Beans ( Phaseolus vulgaris L.): Genetic Control, Differential Expressions of Isozymes, and Sensitivity to Arsenic” [ 1 ]. The article was found to contain images with signs of duplication and manipulation in Figures 1, 3, 4, 5(A), 5(B), 6, 8(b), 9(C), 9(G), 9(I), and 9(J).
