Retraction Note: In situ structural characterization of early amyloid aggregates in Alzheimer’s disease transgenic mice and Octodon degus Retraction of: Scientifc Reports 10.1038/s41598-020-62708-2, published online 03 April 2020 The Authors have retracted this Article. The Authors did not have appropriate permissions for the use of the Octodon degus samples in this study, and therefore retract this Article. Núria Benseny-Cases, Elena Álvarez-Marimon, Ester Aso, Margarita Carmona, Oxana Klementieva, Isidre Ferrer & Josep Cladera agree with the retraction and its wording.
