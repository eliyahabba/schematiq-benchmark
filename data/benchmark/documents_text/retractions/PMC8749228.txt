Retraction: Bactericidal/Permeability-Increasing Protein Improves Cognitive Impairment in Diabetic Mice via Blockade of the LPS-LBP-TLR4 Signaling Pathway The journal and Chief Editors retract the 11 February 2021 article cited above. Following publication, concerns were raised regarding the validity of the data in the article. The authors failed to provide the raw data or a satisfactory explanation during the investigation, which was conducted in accordance with Frontiers' policies. Given the concerns, and the lack of raw data, the editors no longer have confidence in the findings presented in the article. This retraction was approved by the Chief Editors of Frontiers in Physiology and the Chief Executive Editor of Frontiers. The authors agreed with this retraction.
