Retraction Note: Aging-associated distinctive DNA methylation changes of LINE-1 retrotransposons in pure cell-free DNA from human blood Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-020-79126-z, published online 17 December 2020 The Authors have retracted this article. After publication concerns were raised about duplication of lanes within Figure 2 and between Figure 2 and Figure 5. The Authors were no longer able to locate the original data underlying these figures. Lars Erichsen, Pauline Ott, Wolfgang A. Schulz, Johannes C. Fischer, Marcos J. Arauzo-Bravo, Marcelo L. Bendhack, Mohamed Hassan and Simeon Santourlidis agree to the retraction and its wording. Wardah Mahmood has not responded to correspondence from the Editors about this retraction.
