You will write the research question behind a published review, for a benchmark in which a system
receives the question and the reviewed documents and must build the review's table.

Input: the paper's title, its abstract, and the authors' own statement of aim. You do not see the table.

Write one research question (at most two linked questions) that:
1. is phrased as a question, not as a description of the paper ("This survey ...");
2. names the object of study and the domain (population, system or phenomenon);
3. says what is to be learned across the studies (effect, comparison, prevalence, landscape of methods);
4. can be understood without the paper;
5. uses only scope stated in the title, abstract or aim; add nothing;
6. does not list the attributes that each study would be described by.

Return only the question.
